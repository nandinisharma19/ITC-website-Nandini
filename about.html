About ITC

The Institute Technical Council (ITC) at IIT Bombay is a student community that focusses on technology, design, and innovation. We are at the forefront of nurturing student innovation, technical skills, and leadership. Our primary purpose is to provide students with resources and guidance to bring their ideas to life through various technical clubs, teams ,events and communities. ITC plays a pivotal role in organizing technical workshops, competitions, and projects. From astronomy to artificial intelligence, the council caters to a diverse set of technical interests, making it a hub for student-led innovation.

ITC's Goal

The goal of ITC is to empower students with technical skills, encourage innovation, and give them oppurtunity to participate in various tech competitions,hackathons,workshops and seminars. It aims at buiding a tech enthusiasts community, support beginners by conducting workshops and providing them resources to learn.

Activities

workshops by various clubs for both beginners and experienced, tech competitions,hackathons, seminars/lectures by professors, real world projects for tech enthusiasts, trips like stargazing trips organized by krittkia astronomy club of iitb, industry exposure through industry visit, tech teams participate in national and international competitions.
